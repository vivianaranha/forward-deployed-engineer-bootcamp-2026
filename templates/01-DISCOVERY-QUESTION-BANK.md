# Discovery Question Bank

## Business
- What outcome must change?
- What happens if nothing changes?
- Who owns the outcome?

## Workflow
- Walk me through the last real example.
- Where do people wait, copy/paste, rework or approve?

## Data/Technical
- What systems/data are required?
- Who owns access?
- What can we mock?

## Success
- What is the baseline?
- What result would convince leadership?
