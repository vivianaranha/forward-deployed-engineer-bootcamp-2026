# 90-Second Promo Script

**Created by School of AI**

A customer tells you: "We need AI."

What do you build?

A Forward Deployed Engineer does not start with the model. You start with the customer: what workflow is broken, who feels the pain, where do people wait, what systems are involved, what is the baseline, and what can you prove quickly?

This bootcamp covers discovery, architecture, prototyping, AI, RAG, agents, MCP, APIs, integrations, deployment, evaluation, security, observability, adoption, ROI and executive communication.

The capstone gives you a vague customer request and asks you to run the engagement end to end—from discovery to measurable business value.
