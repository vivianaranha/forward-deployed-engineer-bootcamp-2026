# Customer Lab 20 — Forward Deployed Engineer Capstone

**Created by School of AI**

## Customer context
Northstar Services is a synthetic enterprise customer with support, operations and IT workflows.

## Outcome
**Run a complete simulated FDE engagement from discovery to measurable customer outcome.**

## Delivery sequence
1. Review the customer context.
2. Separate known facts from unknowns.
3. Identify the next highest-value discovery question.
4. State the customer outcome.
5. Establish the baseline.
6. Define scope and out-of-scope.
7. List technical dependencies.
8. Rank the top three delivery risks.
9. Build/design the smallest proof.
10. Test a happy path and failure path.
11. Demo to a simulated customer persona.
12. Capture feedback.
13. Change one decision based on evidence.
14. Define the next milestone.
15. Record value evidence.

## Required deliverables
- discovery/technical artifact
- decision log update
- risk register update
- customer-facing summary
- evidence/test output
- next-step recommendation

A strong submission explains not only what was built, but why it was the right thing to build next.
